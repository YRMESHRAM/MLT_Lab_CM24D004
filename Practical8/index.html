How it works
  1. Add classes – type a name (e.g. Person A) and click Add.
  2. Capture images – click 📸 Capture under a class; the camera will auto-snap 10 photos with a 1s gap.
  3. Repeat for every class you want to recognise (min 2).
  4. Train Model – a dense layer on top of MobileNet is trained in the browser.
  5. Predict – the live feed shows real-time classification results.
